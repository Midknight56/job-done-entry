About Job Done

Job Done turns spoken work into an invoice.

It sends the invoice through PayPal sandbox.

The customer can scan and pay before you leave.

Try it

  1. Open the app.
  2. Hold to talk or type a demo line, such as Kitchen tap for Maria at 14 Elm, hour and a half, the tap was 89.
  3. Choose the visit or enter a completed visit. Check its start and end times, then tap Confirm visit.
  4. Tap Send.
  5. Pay on the PayPal sandbox page, signed in as the sandbox buyer named in the project README.

What is real

  • Every invoice is a real PayPal sandbox invoice.
  • Every call appears in Behind the scenes with its HTTP code.
  • The AI never sets a price.

What is limited

  • Sandbox money only.
  • There is a daily free AI budget. After it runs out, recorded model answers are used and labelled.
  • A demo hold lasts 2 minutes.
